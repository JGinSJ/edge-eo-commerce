Prioritized delivery — skinny & prioritized HTML for AI bots

One URL, different payloads by who's asking. A person gets the full page — mega-menus, promos, related links, footer, the works. An AI answer-engine gets a bot-optimized variant: skinny (chrome stripped) or prioritized (chrome stripped and the answer hoisted above the marketing intro). Each is derived from a per-template config — 50 configs → 375k pages — and would run at the edge per bot.
