How to take a screenshot on your Northwind phone

Updated 2 weeks ago · Applies to: Aurora 5, Aurora 6, Aurora 7 · 3 min read · By Northwind Support Team

Capturing what's on your screen is one of the most useful things your Northwind phone can do — whether you're saving a confirmation number, grabbing a boarding pass, sharing a funny message, or keeping a record of an error before you contact support. On your Northwind phone, a screenshot saves an exact image of whatever is on the display at that moment, right into your Photos so you can crop it, mark it up, or share it in seconds.

This guide covers the fastest way to take a screenshot on the Aurora 5, Aurora 6, and Aurora 7, plus how to capture a long, scrolling page, how to edit and annotate your capture, how to share it, and what to do if the shortcut isn't working. If you use accessibility features or a case that covers the side buttons, there are notes for that too. Everything here applies to the current Northwind software; if a step looks different, check for a software update first.

To take a screenshot on your Northwind phone:

  1. Open the screen you want to capture.
  2. Press and hold the Side button and the Volume-down button at the same time, then release both.
  3. The screen flashes and a thumbnail appears in the lower-left corner. Tap it to edit, or swipe it away to save.
  4. Find your screenshot in Photos → Albums → Screenshots.
Aurora 7Aurora 6Aurora 5

Capture a scrolling screenshot

After you take a screenshot, tap the thumbnail in the lower-left corner, then choose Capture more to grab a full, scrolling page — handy for long articles, chat threads, or receipts. Drag the crop handles to set how much of the page to keep, then tap Done to save the full-length image to your Screenshots album.

Edit and mark up a screenshot

Tap the thumbnail right after you capture it to open the editor. From there you can crop, rotate, draw, highlight, or add text. Use the markup tools to circle the important part or hide sensitive information before you share. Tap Done to save your changes to the Screenshots album.

Share a screenshot

Open the screenshot in Photos and tap the Share icon to send it by message, email, or your favorite app. You can also share directly from the thumbnail preview without saving first — useful when you just need to fire off a quick image and move on.

Troubleshooting

If the button combination isn't working, make sure no case is blocking the buttons, and confirm your software is up to date under Settings → Software update. Some accessibility gestures can override the default capture shortcut; if you use AssistiveTouch or a custom gesture, check your accessibility settings. Still stuck? Chat with us or visit a store.
